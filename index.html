Frequently Asked Questions

Click on the "Farmers" page from the navigation, fill in your name, phone number, district, and primary crops. Once submitted, your profile becomes visible to buyers across Uganda.
Visit the Marketplace, browse available produce from verified farmers, and click "Place Order" on any listing. You can compare prices across districts before ordering.
Register your vehicle on the Transport page, then browse open delivery requests. Submit a bid with your price and estimated delivery time. The farmer/buyer selects the best offer.
Basic registration and browsing are free for all users. A small service fee applies only when a transaction is completed through the platform.
